About AU-JRC School Activities Calendar

The AU-JRC School Activities Calendar is an interactive centralized hub designed for students, faculty, and administrative staff at Arellano University - Jose Rizal Campus. It keeps the entire Chiefs community updated with school events, organizational activities, and official notices.

Key Features

  • Interactive Monthly, Weekly, and List Views: Seamlessly switch between different calendar perspectives.
  • Organization Filters: Filter events hosted by SSC, ICT Club, Red Cross, Sports Dev, and more.
  • Real-time Announcements: Stay updated on venue changes, postponements, and schedule alterations.
  • Admin Portal: Authorized moderators can post and manage campus schedules efficiently.
